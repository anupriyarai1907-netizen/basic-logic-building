# Digital Sum and Product

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Estimated Time Limit:** 15 minutes

## Problem Statement

A number-analysis application needs to process the digits of a given positive integer.

Your task is to calculate:

1. The sum of all digits.
2. The product of all digits.
3. Whether the number is divisible by 3 based on its digit sum.

The program must process the number digit by digit using a loop.

## Input Format

A single positive integer N.

## Output Format

Print:

- Sum: <sum>
- Product: <product>
- Divisible by 3: Yes/No

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ N ≤ 10^9

## Example

### Input

234

### Output

Sum: 9
Product: 24
Divisible by 3: Yes

## Explanation

Digits are 2, 3, 4.

Sum:

2 + 3 + 4 = 9

Product:

2 × 3 × 4 = 24

Since 9 is divisible by 3, the number is divisible by 3.
