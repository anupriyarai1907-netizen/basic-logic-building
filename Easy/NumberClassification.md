# Number Classification

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Estimated Time Limit:** 15 minutes

## Problem Statement

A mathematics teacher wants to analyze a positive integer entered by a student.

For the given number, determine whether it is:

- Even or Odd
- Positive or Zero
- Divisible by 5 or not

The program must display all applicable classifications.

## Input Format

A single integer N.

## Output Format

Print three lines:

- Even/Odd
- Positive/Zero
- Divisible by 5/Not Divisible by 5

## Constraints

- 0 ≤ N ≤ 10^9

## Example

### Input

120

### Output

Even
Positive
Divisible by 5

## Test Cases

### Test Case 1

Input: 17

Output:
Odd
Positive
Not Divisible by 5

### Test Case 2

Input: 0

Output:
Even

Divisible by 5
